How PixelForge AI Works

A transparent look at the engine: what runs in your browser, and why results stay natural.

The image is rescaled to 2× / 4× / 8×. AI runs overlapping tiles with blended seams; Classic uses hardware-accelerated canvas.
Edge-local micro-contrast is blended back in. Amount, radius and noise threshold adapt automatically — never oversharpened.
A small lift keeps results lively without garish boosts. Metrics shown (Quality, Sharpness, Detail, Noise) are always real measured values.

Engine in 30 seconds

Classic is instant and safe. AI hallucinates real texture for very small images. Smart Mode picks the level from measured sharpness, noise, detail and size — faces always get gentler handling.

Interview Q&A

AI is heavier (model download + GPU). Classic wins for clean, large images where faithful resampling beats hallucination.
Every Quality / Sharpness / Detail / Noise value is recomputed from pixels before and after — never hardcoded.
AI tiles with overlap blending to avoid out-of-memory; history falls back to in-session-only when localStorage quota is hit.
